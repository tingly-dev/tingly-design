Editor shell

Every kind is the same frame with different contents. Before giving a kind its own chrome, find its slot here. Every side column (right panel, slide strip, tabs, comments, navigation, data sources) is resized by dragging its inner edge, and with the prompt on the left the whole frame sits to the right of the conversation column. No frame is drawn until a project’s files say which kind it is; a project that cannot open says why, with All projects under it.
