1
Edit where it is drawn
Double-click retypes copy that has holes. The change lands in the one piece it touches: a value the hole shows, or the words around it. The hole is never replaced.
2
Loops
Rows of a list declared in data-props are edited per row; literal words inside a loop change every item (the “repeated” notice already says so).
3
Why not
Text fed only by the script says so on double-click, with a way out. An edit that spans several pieces is refused with a reason and the artboard is drawn as before.
4
Colour field
A colour that is one hole shows the real colour and its tweak’s name; editing it edits the tweak. Contrast uses the real colour.
5
Tweaks lists everything
A list is a read-only row that points at the artboard, never a text box that would write the list back as a string.
6
Canvas only
Slides, motion and the other kinds have no holes.
