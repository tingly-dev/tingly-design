Why this shape
Three rows, ordered by how often they are used. Middle: the text, the thing people came to do, with room to breathe. Top: what the request is about, plus three quiet icon buttons (history, position, fold) that look like nothing until needed. Bottom: how it goes. Attach and the settings of this request on the left, who answers and Send on the right.
One height for everything that can be clicked in the bottom row (28 to 32 px), one radius for chips (pill), one accent for state (blue = on / ready). The segmented switch is neutral, so the only blue on the card is the thing that matters now. Send is a round button: grey while empty, blue when it can go, an outlined square while running.
Nothing that does not apply is drawn: Fast AI has no conversation or picture chip, a project with no image model has no ImageGen chip, a dashboard and a design system have neither.
