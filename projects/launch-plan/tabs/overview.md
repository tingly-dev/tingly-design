# Meridian Forecast — Launch plan

Owner: Priya Raman · Status: In review · Target GA: November 4, 2026.

Meridian Forecast gives finance and RevOps teams a driver-based revenue forecast built on the metrics they already trust in Meridian. This plan covers scope, success measures, rollout and the open decisions before launch.

## Why we are building it

Most of our Scale customers forecast ARR in a spreadsheet that is rebuilt by hand each month from Stripe and Salesforce exports. The numbers drift from what Meridian reports, and nobody can say which assumption moved the forecast. Forecast replaces that spreadsheet with a model that reads the same metrics layer as our dashboards and shows the driver behind every change.

> “Our board deck and our forecast should never disagree. Today they disagree every month.” — VP Finance, Scale customer, beta interview
