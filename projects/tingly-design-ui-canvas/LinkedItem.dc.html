Linked pages: the item

A linked page is an ordinary element (a picture, or an <x-embed> where the page can run) plus a record of where it came from, and the picture drawn of it is kept beside it. Selecting it opens one form; it is never edited from inside. See .design/cross-kind-embedding.md.
