The conversation and the left side

Today the conversation can be a column on the left. In Slides, Docs and a design system that column is where the project’s own list lives, so the list and the conversation can never be on screen together; in the other kinds the work sits between two side columns. Recommended: the conversation becomes a right-hand pane (B) and the left edge stays the kind’s. Design note: .design/chat-pane.md · issue #463.

The conversation costs one column, not a third. In Canvas, Docs, Dashboard and Motion a whole side column goes away at the same time (628 → 940 at 1280 in Canvas).
A · A left rail, one panel at a time
A 48 px icon rail at the left edge (pages, tabs, list, conversation); one panel opens at a time. Both are always findable and take no extra width.
Still: the list and the conversation cannot be seen together, the work is still between two columns, and four kinds get a two-level sidebar for two or three items.
B · The conversation is a right paneRecommended
The left edge always belongs to the kind. The conversation is a pane beside Properties, Comments or Sources — one right column at a time — and the request bar’s position menu goes away.
Cost: the conversation cannot be open beside Properties; a design system, whose screen was using the left column for its list, loses 220 px (940 → 720 at 1280).
C · Move the kind’s own list out of the left
Pages become a filmstrip at the bottom, tabs a bar at the top, a design system’s list goes into the right or a menu; the left is the conversation’s.
Still: three new layouts and three sets of narrow rules, the work is still squeezed by three columns (worse in Docs and Dashboard), and each list leaves what it lists.
