Pick a page and it lands on the current slide, selected, with its options in the right panel. Nothing asks “picture or live” here: the default is live where this kind can show it, a picture otherwise, and the panel changes it.
What the picker never does
Dashboards and design systems do not take a page, so they have no entry; a page that this kind cannot hold is greyed with the reason (for example, a dashboard in a motion piece: “Motion renders frame by frame; a dashboard is not a function of time. Its picture can go in.”).
