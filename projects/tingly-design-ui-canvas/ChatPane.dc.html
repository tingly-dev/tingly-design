The conversation pane

The recommended option in three states: the bar at the bottom (default), the conversation in a right pane, and a window too narrow to pin it. The left edge is always the kind’s own list; the pane is chosen from the top bar, like Properties, Comments or Sources.

“Work” is the stage, the doc page or the main area, with the right pane open. A design system is the one kind that gives up width: its list used to hide behind the conversation, and now it stays. The doc page stops at its 760 px cap, so extra width is margin.
Rules
1One right column. The conversation is a pane like Properties, Comments or Sources; at most one is open.
2The left edge is the kind’s. Pages, tabs and a design system’s list never fold into the conversation.
3No position menu. The bar is at the bottom by default and at the pane’s foot when it is open; the saved left mode is migrated once.
4A draft survives. Moving between the bottom and the pane, closing it and reopening it never lose typed words (#258).
5A narrow window says why. The toggle is disabled with a reason in its hint; nothing moves by itself and the choice stays.
6The top bar is whole again. It spans the window, and the conversation’s run status dot moves onto its toggle.
