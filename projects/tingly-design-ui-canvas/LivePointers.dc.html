Claude Code is violet, the fast AI teal, outside the editor pink; people take a colour from a palette by window.

4
Docs: the cursor rests at the lower right corner of the first top-level block that changed, its name to the left (the page margin is too narrow on the right); it follows the text as it reflows and scrolls with the page.
Never takes a click, never moves the view or the selection, never in an export. No switch: nothing shows unless someone else is changing the page.
