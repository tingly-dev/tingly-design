1
Top bar
The name, size (one segment per format), speed, notes, check, Import / Export, the right panel toggle — the same bar as every kind.
2
Stage
The frame at the playhead. Paused, it is editable like a slide. The selected element is drawn landed (“landed” tag) so a drag decides where it settles; everything else stays at its moment.
3
Timeline
Play, time, “+ Scene”; the ruler; one block per scene (right edge drags its length; right-click: add, duplicate, move, delete; ◆ marks a transition). Below, a row per animated element of the scene under the playhead: the bar runs from entrance to exit — head is the entrance preset, tail the exit. Drag the middle to slide both, an edge to move or stretch one; drag snaps to the playhead, scene bounds, other elements and 0.1 s (Alt frees). A dashed left edge is an automatic start; once dragged a real @time. ▸ opens the keyframe tracks; a diamond drags to move that key. “Elements N” in the top row folds the rows away (the default, most pieces never need them); open, the rows keep one height whatever the scene holds (empty scene: one line of text), and the band’s bottom edge drags to change it (double-click resets); both are remembered in the browser.
4
Right panel
Layers (off-screen faded, with when they enter), where a style change lands, the style rows, and “Animation”: preset and easing for Enter and Leave, length and @start, and the keyframe tracks (◆ with a count, ◇ to start one at the playhead). A tracked property is written as the key at the playhead, not as style; timing is shared by every size.
5
Agent bar
Targets the scene or the selected element; fast AI writes the same timing attributes by hand.
Open
Hovering a preset could play it on the stage once (needs a runtime channel). Dragging scene blocks to reorder is menu-only. Text and shape insertion at the playhead. Installing a design system into a piece (phase 4).
