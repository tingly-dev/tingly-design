1
One card per theme
Every theme of every design system installed here, plus Original look. A click shows the whole work in it: no preview step, no apply button. The lit card is the theme most of the work’s colours are now.
2
Undo is the way back
The toast says how many places changed and carries Undo; ⌘Z undoes the whole switch too. Original look puts back what the first switch replaced (the page, text and accent colours the work had); it is off when the colours were already a system’s.
3
Colours by role
The worn theme’s tokens grouped as surfaces, text, lines, accents, status. They are written as plain values, so Claude Code and git see ordinary colours, not variables.
4
Contrast
Each text colour against the surface it is drawn on; a pair under its limit is tinted, never blocked.
5
Deck scope
A deck says whether the switch is for every slide or the open one. “This slide” leaves the deck’s theme record alone.
6
What stays
Colours that are not any system’s stay as they were, and the line under the cards says how many. Claude Code can be asked to restyle them (the offer under the request bar).
Open
Where this lives once project settings move into one place (see Interaction consistency). A canvas keeps its typefaces and text styles; only a deck follows the theme’s typefaces.
